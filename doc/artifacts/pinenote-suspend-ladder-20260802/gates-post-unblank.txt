G2 is CLOSED when the primary plane shows fb=0 or crtc=(null):
drm_atomic_helper_dirtyfb() then commits an empty state and returns 0.

It is ALSO closed, less obviously, when the plane holds a *different*
fb than the fbdev one -- e.g. a KMS compositor owning the plane. The
dirtyfb loop matches on identity, not on 'some plane is lit'. So the
owner of the plane's fb is the reading that matters:

== G4  fbcon binding (the thing that normally repaints after un-suspend)
vtcon0: bind=1  name=(S) dummy device
vtcon1: bind=0  name=(M) frame buffer device
bind=0 for the frame buffer device means nothing regenerates damage
after fb_set_suspend(0) -- fbcon_resumed() returns early with no
console attached. The reader image runs that way by design; note it
only matters when the fbdev path is what drives the panel at all.

== supporting counters
defio_delay_ms = 250
EBC IRQs       = 970
irq/66-fdec0000.ebc (116) state=S (sleeping)
ebc-refresh/fdec0000.ebc (117) state=I (idle)

Reading order: G1 first (one bit, decisive), then G3 (an open card0
invalidates any conclusion drawn from an unblank or a set_par), then
G2, then G4.  G3 must be checked before trusting a recovery attempt.
